# E-Commerce Refund Policy (Strict)

This policy governs all refund decisions. The support agent must follow these
rules exactly and must not make exceptions beyond what is written here.

## Summary: When a Refund Is Approved

A refund is **approved** only when the order has been delivered, has not
already been refunded, is not a final-sale/clearance item, AND at least one
of these is true:
- The item is **unused** and it has been **7 days or fewer** since delivery, OR
- The item is confirmed **defective** and it has been **14 days or fewer**
  since delivery, OR
- The item is a **digital product**, has **not been accessed**, and it has
  been **5 days or fewer** since delivery.

If none of these apply, the refund is **denied**. The detailed rules below
explain exactly why.

## 1. Standard Return Window
Refunds are only accepted within **7 days** of the delivery date. Requests
made after day 7 are denied — unless Rule 5 (Manufacturer Defects) applies.

## 2. Item Condition
The item must be **unused** and in its original condition. Items reported as
**damaged by the customer** are **never** eligible for a refund, regardless
of how recently they were delivered.

## 3. Final Sale / Clearance Items
Items marked as **final sale** or **clearance** are **non-refundable**,
regardless of condition or time since delivery. This rule overrides every
other rule.

## 4. Digital Products
Digital products (courses, downloads, licenses) are refundable **only if**:
- Delivered within the last **5 days**, **and**
- Not yet accessed/downloaded by the customer.

Once a digital product has been accessed, it becomes non-refundable
immediately, regardless of how recently it was delivered.

## 5. Manufacturer Defects (Exception to Rule 1)
If the item is confirmed **defective** (a manufacturing fault, not customer
damage), the customer is eligible for a full refund within **14 days** of
delivery — overriding the standard 7-day window.

## 6. One Refund Per Order
An order that has **already been refunded** cannot be refunded again.

## 7. Undelivered Orders
Orders that have not yet been delivered (status: `shipped` or `processing`)
cannot be refunded. The customer must request a **cancellation** instead —
a separate process this agent does not handle.

## 8. Refund Amount
- Standard or digital refunds: full item price.
- Defective-item refunds: full item price **plus** shipping fees.
- All other approved refunds: shipping fees are non-refundable.
